How the savings are calculated

All figures are estimates until school-level loading data is added.

Each school's government funding is based on the Schooling Resource Standard: a base amount for each primary and secondary student, plus extra loadings for students and schools with greater needs.

For a student at a government school, the NSW Government pays 75% of that student's Schooling Resource Standard. For a student at a Catholic school, it pays 21.4%, after deducting the amount parents are expected to contribute.

A school's saving to NSW taxpayers is the difference between those two amounts, added up across all of its students.
